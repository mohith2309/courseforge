## 2024-05-18 - Entitlement Spoofing in Claim and Pro Endpoints
**Vulnerability:** The `/me/pro` and `/me/claim` endpoints implicitly trusted client-provided state. `/me/pro` allowed any signed-in user to grant themselves "Pro" status simply by making a POST request. `/me/claim` accepted an arbitrary array of `courseIds` and an optional `pro` boolean from the client body and applied them directly to the user's entitlements, enabling users to claim paid courses or grant themselves "Pro" status without payment.
**Learning:** Client-provided entitlement state or billing signals (like claiming a course ID or passing `pro: true`) should never be blindly trusted. The source of truth for pricing and entitlements must always reside on the server.
**Prevention:** Remove direct entitlement granting endpoints (`/me/pro`). Harden migration endpoints (`/me/claim`) to explicitly load the items being claimed (e.g., from the `published` catalog) and verify server-side that their price is exactly 0 before granting access. Ensure all paid content acquisition routes strictly through the secure payment flow (`/payments/*`).
